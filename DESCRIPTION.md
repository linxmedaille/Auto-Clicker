# Autoclicker: descriptions

Ready-to-paste text for a repository, download page or app listing. Every claim here
matches how the app currently behaves.

## Tagline

> A precise, portable autoclicker for Windows: one small exe, F6 to start, out of the way in the tray.

## Short (repository "About", ≤ 160 characters)

> Portable Windows autoclicker in C: drift-free timing from 1 ms, custom hotkey, fixed-point clicking, tray mode. One ~535 KB exe, no installer.

## Medium (download page or store summary)

> **Autoclicker** clicks for you, precisely. Set the interval, choose the mouse button and click
> type, then press F6 from any app. It clicks at your cursor or at a fixed point you pick on
> screen, until you stop it or for a set number of clicks.
>
> Timing is scheduled against a high-resolution clock, so the rate doesn't drift, whether
> that's one click every few hours or one every millisecond. It never clicks its own window, so starting it with the mouse
> is safe. Close it and it keeps running in the system tray.
>
> It's a single portable exe of about 535 KB, with no installer, no extra files and no network
> access. It looks sharp at any display scaling and follows your light or dark theme.

## Feature list

- Interval in milliseconds or seconds, from 1 ms to 24 hours, without drift
- Left, right or middle button; single or double click
- Click until stopped, or a fixed number of clicks
- Click at the cursor, or at a fixed point picked on screen
- Start/stop hotkey (F6 by default), changeable to any key or combination
- Esc stops instantly while clicking
- Holds off while the cursor is over its own window, so it never clicks its own Stop button
- Live click counter and status: idle, running or waiting
- Closes to the system tray; one copy at a time
- Single portable `.exe`, about 535 KB; no installer, no extra DLLs
- No network requests and no data collection; saves only your hotkey
- Crisp at every Windows display scaling level; light and dark themes
- Respects Windows reduced-motion, transparency and contrast settings; fully keyboard-operable

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (built into Windows 11 and present
on almost all up-to-date Windows 10 PCs).
