# Auto Clicker
Autoclicker A small, precise autoclicker for Windows 10 and 11. One portable autoclicker.exe (about 535 KB): no installer, no extra DLLs, nothing to set up. Download it, run it, press F6.  Accurate timing. Clicks are scheduled against a high-resolution clock, so the rate doesn't drift, from one click every few hours down to 1 ms. Can't click itself. While the cursor is over the Autoclicker window it waits, so pressing Start never clicks its own Stop button. Out of the way. Close the window and it keeps running in the system tray; the hotkey still works from any app. Your hotkey. F6 by default, or any key or combination you choose. Sharp on any display. Renders at your screen's real resolution at every Windows scaling level, and follows your light or dark theme.
